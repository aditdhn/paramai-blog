ParamAI™ started with a simple observation: Indian students get career advice
shaped more by family pressure and dated stereotypes than by evidence. We
built an AI assessment that matches you to **1,500+ real careers** across
**41 sectors**, grounded in dual RIASEC + OCEAN psychometrics and a
career knowledge bank.

This blog is the long-form companion. You’ll find:

- **Sector guides.** Guides to the sectors in the ParamAI™ knowledge bank,
  published as each one is ready: who tends to thrive there, how people get
  in, and what the realistic paths look like.
- **Assessment science.** The research behind the questions: what RIASEC and
  OCEAN actually measure, why we use adaptive item response theory, and how
  we avoid the failure modes that plague quiz-based "career tests."
- **Student and parent guides.** Practical writing for families making real
  decisions — stream selection, entrance prep, first-job transitions.

No affiliate links. No sponsored rankings. Just the research we use to build
the product.

## How we handle numbers

When we quote a figure, we say where it comes from and what it does not show.
Pay is always indicative context for exploring careers, never a promise of what
a job will pay. Our [numbers behind the careers](/data/) page shows what the
national labour survey says about pay in the highest-paying career fields.
